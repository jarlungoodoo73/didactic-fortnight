## What is Philosophy?

Philosophy (from Greek φιλοσοφία, philosophia, 'love of wisdom') is the study of fundamental questions about existence, knowledge, values, reason, mind, and language.

## Major Branches

### Epistemology - Theory of Knowledge
What can we know? How do we know what we know?

Key questions:
- What is truth?
- How do we acquire knowledge?
- What is the relationship between belief and knowledge?

### Metaphysics - Nature of Reality
What is real? What is the nature of existence?

The classic thought experiment: **Plato's Cave**
- We may only see shadows of reality
- True knowledge requires breaking free from illusions
- Reality might be fundamentally different from our perceptions

### Ethics - Moral Philosophy
What should we do? What is the good life?

Different approaches:
- **Utilitarianism**: Maximum happiness for maximum people
- **Deontology**: Duty-based ethics (Kant)
- **Virtue Ethics**: Character and excellence (Aristotle)

### Logic - The Art of Reasoning
How do we reason correctly?

A simple syllogism:
1. All humans are mortal (major premise)
2. Socrates is human (minor premise)
3. Therefore, Socrates is mortal (conclusion)

This connects to [[Mathematics|mathematical logic]] and [[Programming|computational logic]].

## Eastern Philosophy

### Buddhism - The Middle Way
Key concepts:
- The Four Noble Truths
- The Eightfold Path
- Impermanence (Anicca)
- Non-self (Anatta)

### Taoism - The Way
> "The journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step." — Lao Tzu

The Tao emphasizes:
- Natural flow and balance
- Wu Wei (effortless action)
- Harmony with nature

## Philosophical Paradoxes

### The Ship of Theseus
If you replace all parts of a ship one by one, is it still the same ship?

This relates to:
- Identity over time
- What makes something "the same"
- The nature of continuity

### Zeno's Paradox
To reach a destination, you must first get halfway there. But to get halfway, you must get a quarter of the way... and so on infinitely.

This ancient paradox touches on:
- Infinity and limits
- The nature of motion
- [[Mathematics|Calculus]] (which resolves it!)

### The Trolley Problem
A trolley is heading toward five people. You can pull a lever to divert it to a track with one person. What do you do?

This explores moral philosophy and ethical decision-making.

## Modern Philosophy

### Existentialism
"Existence precedes essence" - Sartre

Key ideas:
- We create our own meaning
- Radical freedom and responsibility
- Authenticity

### Philosophy of Mind
The mind-body problem:
- How does consciousness arise from physical matter?
- What is the nature of subjective experience?
- Free will vs. determinism

This intersects with [[Cognitive Science]] and [[Artificial Intelligence]].
