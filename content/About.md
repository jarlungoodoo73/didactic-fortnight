## The Philosophy

This garden follows the principles of [[Digital Gardening]]:

🌱 **Seeds**: Early ideas, rough notes, works in progress
🌿 **Budding**: Developing thoughts that are taking shape  
🌲 **Evergreen**: Mature, well-developed concepts

Notes are **living documents** - they grow, change, and improve over time.
